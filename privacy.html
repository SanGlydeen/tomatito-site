Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Tomatito doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data. There's no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your timer settings, the timer itself and your harvest (how many tomatoes you've finished each day) are stored only on your own devices. Nobody else, including us, can see them.

iPhone and Apple Watch

  • Your iPhone and Apple Watch keep each other in sync directly, using Apple's built-in connection between the two. Nothing passes through our servers. We don't have any.
  • Alerts are scheduled on your device. They aren't sent from the internet.
  • If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Tomatito, your voice request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Mac

The Mac app works entirely offline and never connects to the internet.

Web version and this website

  • The web version saves your timer and settings in your browser's own storage on your device.
  • To show its typeface, the web version loads a font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when it first opens. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • This website and the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • This website uses no cookies.

Children

Tomatito is suitable for everyone and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
